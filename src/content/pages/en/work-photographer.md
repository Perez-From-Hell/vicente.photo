## Fine Art and Commercial Photography

As a photographer, I combine technical mastery of lighting and camera mechanics with a keen eye for authenticity and emotion.

### Graduation Class Composites
Capturing individual portraits of students with dedicated studio lighting setups for subsequent digital compositing in Photoshop, alongside location imagery of the academic institution.

![Graduation portrait](/img/work-photographer-orla.jpg)
Technical parameters for each portrait: ISO 250, F8, 1/100

### Graduation Ceremonies
Comprehensive coverage of graduation ceremonies and receptions, guiding graduates for posed portraits and capturing candid milestones.

![Graduation](/img/work-photographer-graduation.jpg)
Technical parameters: ISO 250, F8, 1/100

### Live Events Photography
On-stage coverage of concerts, cultural performances, and live events, capturing lighting dynamics and spontaneous energy.
