## Photography with soul and technique

My passion for the image ranges from building my own camera to the patient observation of my surroundings. I understand photography not merely as pressing a shutter, but as an integral process where technique must always serve emotion. With each exposure, I seek to transcend the image to capture the atmosphere and living essence of the moment.

My commitment is to build a solid and distinctive visual narrative, ensuring each photograph tells its own story and speaks for itself.

Photograph taken of a guitarist in the downtown streets of Porto, with a deep gaze that conveyed both charisma and affection for his favorite instrument.

## Photographic technique

My work is based on expression through both digital and analog photography. One of my greatest technical strengths is the design and construction of my own large format camera. This handcrafted process has granted me a deep understanding of camera mechanics, where I decided every last parameter of exposure when taking a photograph.
