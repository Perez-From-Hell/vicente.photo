## Project: Street Panoramas
I wanted to combine the striking aspect ratio that a panorama brings with the vibrant, detail-rich life on the street, offering another perspective of urban spaces. The photos were composed from 3 images captured on the spot and stitched together in Photoshop.

![Artist at work](/img/proyects-street-panoramas-001.jpg)
Technical parameters: ISO 250, F8, 1/100
