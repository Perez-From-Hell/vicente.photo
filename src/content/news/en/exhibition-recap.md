## A Wonderful Evening

Thank you to everyone who joined us for the opening night of "Light & Form". It was deeply rewarding to see familiar faces and engage with fellow art and photography enthusiasts.

The dialogue between large format photography and the gallery space created a distinctive tension and atmosphere. We are already preparing new collaborative projects for next season.
