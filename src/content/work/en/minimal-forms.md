## The Essence of Reduction

In this series, I explored the concept of reduction—stripping away the unnecessary to reveal the essential form. The composition is left relatively unadorned, allowing the silhouette and light to speak for itself.

> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." - Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

The process involved patient observation and deliberate composition to achieve harmony between subject and negative space.
