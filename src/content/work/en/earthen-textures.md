## Surface and Depth

This collection focuses on the tactile and atmospheric experience of the image. Through fine grain and natural lighting, each surface becomes an evocative landscape of its own.

The capture process emphasizes tonal depth, revealing subtle and beautiful nuances in color, texture, and shadows.
