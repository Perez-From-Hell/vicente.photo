## Scale & Presence

This commission involved creating a series of large-format photographic works for an architectural loft space in New York. The goal was to produce images that seamlessly connected with the space's minimalist geometry and natural lighting.

The prints were crafted on archival cotton rag papers using mineral pigments to ensure longevity and tonal fidelity.
